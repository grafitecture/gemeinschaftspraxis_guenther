Dr. med. Andreas Günther

Specialist in internal medicine and gastroenterology. Additional qualification in emergency medicine.

Most recently, Dr. Andreas Günther was senior consultant (Leitender Oberarzt) in the Department of Gastroenterology, Diabetology and Oncology at the Theresienkrankenhaus in Mannheim. In 2014 he took over his father’s practice and has run it together with Dr. Evelyne Günther ever since.

Examine thoroughly, explain clearly – and guide you safely through the healthcare system when specialists or a hospital are needed.

Areas of focus

Stomach, bowel and liver

As a gastroenterologist, he is your contact for problems of the stomach, bowel, liver and pancreas – with ultrasound directly in the practice.

Diabetes and metabolism

He brings many years of hospital experience in diabetology. In the practice he also supports people with diabetes in the structured disease management programme (DMP).

Emergency medicine

With his additional qualification in emergency medicine, he is trained for acute situations – in the practice and on home visits.
