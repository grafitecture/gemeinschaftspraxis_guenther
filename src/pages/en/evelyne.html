Dr. med. Evelyne Günther

Specialist in internal medicine. Hypertension specialist (DHL), acupuncture, palliative medicine.

Before setting up practice in Viernheim in 2014, Dr. Evelyne Günther worked for many years in cardiology, angiology and internal intensive care at the Theresienkrankenhaus in Mannheim. Heart, blood vessels and blood pressure remain her focus to this day – complemented by acupuncture and palliative medicine.

Areas of focus

Heart, circulation and high blood pressure

As a hypertension specialist certified by the German Hypertension League (DHL), she focuses on diagnosing and treating high blood pressure – with 24-hour blood pressure monitoring, ECG and carotid ultrasound directly in the practice.

Acupuncture and TCM

With an additional qualification since 2014: for pain syndromes such as migraine or neck problems, and for allergies, dizziness and tinnitus.

Palliative medicine

With an additional specialist title since 2022: caring for people with serious illness and their families – to relieve symptoms and preserve quality of life.
