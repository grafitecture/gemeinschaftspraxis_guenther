Vaccination schedule

Which vaccinations Germany’s Standing Committee on Vaccination (STIKO) recommends for adults – by age and situation, at a glance.

No appointment needed: come by during consultation hours and bring your vaccination record – we will be glad to check it.

All adultsFrom 60From 75Pre-existing conditionsPregnancyYoung peopleTravel

from 18

For all adults

  • Tetanus and diphtheriabooster every 10 years
  • Whooping coughonce, together with the next tetanus and diphtheria booster
  • Measles, mumps, rubellaonce for everyone born after 1970 whose protection is unclear or who had no more than one dose as a child

from 60

From age 60

  • Fluevery autumn, with a vaccine designed for older adults
  • Pneumococcusonce
  • Shingles2 doses, 2 to 6 months apart

from 75

From age 75

  • RSVonce, ideally in late summer or autumn
  • COVID-19every year, ideally in late summer or early autumn

The vaccinations from 60 still apply.

any age

With pre-existing conditions

If you have a chronic illness – for example of the heart, lungs or kidneys, or diabetes – or a weakened immune system, some vaccinations make sense earlier.

  • Fluevery autumn
  • COVID-19every year with certain underlying conditions
  • Pneumococcusdepending on the condition, also before age 60
  • Shinglesfrom 18 with a weakened immune system or a severe chronic illness, 2 doses
  • RSVonce from 60 with a severe underlying condition or if you live in a care home

We will discuss with you which vaccinations are right for you – for example against hepatitis B as well.

pregnancy

During pregnancy

  • Flufrom the second trimester, in the first if you have an underlying condition
  • Whooping coughin every pregnancy at the start of the third trimester, earlier if a premature birth is likely

Both vaccinations also protect your baby in the first months of life.

9 to 24

Young people

  • Tetanus, diphtheria, whooping cough, poliobooster between 9 and 16
  • HPV2 doses at 9 to 14, catch-up until 17
  • Meningococcus ACWYonce at 12 to 14, catch-up until the 25th birthday

travel

Before you travel

We advise on travel vaccinations. Ideally come six to eight weeks before you leave – some vaccinations need several appointments.

Status: STIKO recommendations 2026 (Epidemiologisches Bulletin 4/2026), COVID-19 recommendation updated in July 2026. This overview does not replace medical advice.
