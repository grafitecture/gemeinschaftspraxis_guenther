Services

Family medicine and internal medicine: from examination to treatment. And if you need a specialist or a hospital, we coordinate your further care.

Examinations and diagnostics

We can clarify a lot directly in the practice.

  • Ultrasound of the abdominal and chest organs, thyroid and carotid artery
  • Resting, exercise and 24-hour ECG
  • 24-hour blood pressure monitoring
  • Lung function test
  • Laboratory tests
  • Dementia screening, e.g. Mini-Mental State Examination

Check-ups and early detection

Finding early what can be treated well.

  • Health check-up, every three years from age 35
  • Skin cancer screening, every two years from age 35
  • Cancer screening for men from age 45
  • Youth health examination J1 (age 12 to 14)
  • Occupational health examination for young workers
  • Fitness for sport, e.g. diving and marathons

Chronic conditions

Long-term support, regular check-ups.

  • Disease management programmes (DMP) for coronary heart disease, diabetes, COPD and asthma
  • Nutrition and dietary advice
  • Infusion therapy and restorative treatments
  • Coordination with specialists and hospitals

Vaccinations and travel

Protected all year round – and on holiday.

  • Vaccinations as recommended by STIKO, no appointment needed
  • Travel vaccination advice
  • Vaccination record check, with reminders on request

Acupuncture and TCM

Complementing conventional medicine.

  • Acupuncture for pain syndromes such as migraine or neck problems
  • Acupuncture for allergies, dizziness and tinnitus
  • Other methods of Traditional Chinese Medicine
  • Autologous blood therapy

At home, in care homes and more

When getting to the practice is not possible.

  • Home visits by arrangement
  • Care in retirement and nursing homes
  • Minor surgery, wound care, aftercare following operations
  • Medical reports, e.g. for pensions, disability and rehabilitation

The practice

We want to be your first point of contact for all health questions – for the long term and as partners. We see the whole person, with family, work and life circumstances. Prevention is especially close to our hearts.

At Kreuzstraße 13 since 1970

Most recently renovated in 2019 and 2023: new reception, new rooms, a new entrance and a new façade.

  1. 1970

    Dr. Bernhard Kleine-Allekotte opens a practice for pulmonology and internal medicine at Kreuzstraße 13.

  2. 1982

    Dr. Michael Günther takes over and runs it for more than 32 years as a family practice with a focus on internal medicine.

  3. Jan 2014

    Dr. Evelyne Günther joins and founds a joint practice with Dr. Michael Günther.

  4. July 2014

    Dr. Andreas Günther takes over his father’s statutory health insurance licence. Since then, Dr. Evelyne and Dr. Andreas Günther have run the practice together.

  5. 2019

    Renovation: new reception, new waiting room, new corridors.

  6. 2023

    New rooms, a new entrance and a new façade with the blue G.
